Shaivonte Aician Gilgeous-Alexander (/ˈʃeɪ/ SHAY;[1] born July 12, 1998), 
also known by his initials SGA,
is a Canadian professional basketball player 
for the Oklahoma City Thunder of the National Basketball 
Association (NBA). He is a four-time NBA All-Star, 
a four-time All-NBA First Team member, and two-time 
NBA Most Valuable Player (MVP). Gilgeous-Alexander 
led the Thunder to their first NBA championship since
relocating from Seattle to Oklahoma City and was named Finals MVP.
